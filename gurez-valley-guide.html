Introduction to Gurez Valley

Situated in the Bandipora district at an altitude of approximately 8,000 feet, Gurez Valley is one of the most serene offbeat destinations in North Kashmir. Located along the historic Silk Route and bounded by the Kishanganga River, Gurez offers landscapes, traditional wooden architecture, and the distinct Dard-Shina culture.

Top Attractions in Gurez

  • Habba Khatoon Peak & Spring: A distinctive pyramid-shaped mountain named after the legendary 16th-century poetess.
  • Dawar Town: The main administrative center of Gurez, providing central amenities, homestays, and market facilities.
  • Razdan Pass: Standing at 11,672 feet, this pass connects Bandipora with Gurez, presenting views of the Harmukh mountain range.
  • Tulail Valley: An untouched sub-valley featuring log-cabin hamlets such as Sheikhpura and Bagtore.

Travel Details, Permits & Accessibility

Route: Srinagar to Bandipora (65 km), then upward over Razdan Pass to Dawar (58 km), totaling around 123 km.

Best Time: May to October. Razdan Pass remains closed during winter due to heavy snow accumulation.

Permit Requirements: Travelers must carry original Government ID proofs (Aadhaar/Passport) to register at security checkpoints along the route.

Frequently asked questions

How to reach Gurez Valley from Srinagar?

Gurez is approximately 123 km from Srinagar via Bandipora and Razdan Pass, taking 5 to 6 hours by road.

Do tourists need a permit for Gurez Valley?

Yes, due to its proximity to the Line of Control (LoC), Indian tourists need to register at police checkpoints en route or obtain local registration.

Gulmarg Travel Planning: What to Check Before You Go

  • Check the current weather forecast for Gulmarg and the higher mountain areas.
  • Confirm current Gondola operating status and ticket information.
  • For winter trips, check current snow and road conditions.
  • If skiing or snowboarding, arrange suitable equipment and qualified local guidance where necessary.
  • Carry clothing appropriate to the season and elevation.
  • Keep plans flexible because mountain weather can affect activities.
  • Do not assume old prices, timings or availability are still current.

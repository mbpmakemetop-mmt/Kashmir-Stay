Welcome to Gulmarg

Gulmarg, located in the Baramulla district at an elevation of 8,694 feet, is Kashmir’s premier year-round alpine resort. Famous for its vast wildflower meadows in summer and world-class skiing slopes in winter, Gulmarg attracts travelers from across the globe.

Top Attractions & Experiences

  • Gulmarg Gondola: One of the highest cable cars in the world, split into Phase 1 (Kungdoor) and Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak at 13,780 feet).
  • Winter Sports & Skiing: Between December and March, Gulmarg becomes a hub for powder skiing, snowboarding, and snowshoeing.
  • Historical Landmarks: Visit Maharani Temple, St. Mary’s Church, and the historic Maharaja Cottage.
  • Drung Waterfall: A popular nearby cascade that completely freezes into ice cascades during peak winter.

Planning Your Gulmarg Journey

Distance: 51 km from Srinagar (approx. 1.5 to 2 hours drive via Tangmarg).

Seasons: April–June for temperate green views and blooming flowers; December–March for thick snow cover and ski activities.

Srinagar to Gulmarg Distance, Route & Travel Time

Gulmarg is one of the easiest day-trip additions to a Srinagar stay — close enough for a half-day visit, far enough to feel like a completely different world. Here's the actual distance, road conditions, and how most people get there.

Quick Facts

Distance~51 km
Travel time by road1.5 - 2 hours
RouteSrinagar → Tangmarg → Gulmarg
Road typePaved throughout, winding in the final stretch
Best forDay trip or overnight stay

The Route

The drive from Srinagar to Gulmarg runs through Magam and Tangmarg before climbing up to Gulmarg itself. The road is paved the whole way and well-used since Gulmarg is one of Kashmir's most visited destinations year-round. The final stretch from Tangmarg to Gulmarg is the steepest and most winding part of the journey — around 13 km of switchbacks gaining significant elevation, which is also where the scenery starts to shift dramatically from valley farmland to pine forest.

Many taxis stop briefly in Tangmarg, especially in winter, since some vehicles without proper snow tires or chains aren't allowed to continue further up when conditions are icy — you may need to switch to a local vehicle equipped for the final climb during heavy snow periods.

How to Get There

By Private Taxi

The most common and convenient option. A one-way private taxi typically costs ₹1,800-2,800 depending on vehicle type (hatchback vs SUV) and season. Most visitors book a round trip with a few hours of wait time built in, which usually costs more than a simple one-way but saves the hassle of arranging a return vehicle from Gulmarg itself.

By Shared Taxi

Shared sumos/taxis run between Srinagar and Tangmarg regularly, and from Tangmarg you can find local shared vehicles up to Gulmarg. This is significantly cheaper than a private taxi but takes longer due to stops and waiting for the vehicle to fill up.

By Bus

J&K SRTC runs buses on this route, but frequency is limited and schedules aren't always convenient for tourists working around gondola timings or day-trip plans. Most visitors skip the bus option in favor of a taxi given the modest price difference and much greater flexibility.

Self-Drive

If you've rented a car in Srinagar, the route is straightforward and well-signposted. Just be cautious on the Tangmarg-Gulmarg stretch in winter — carry chains if driving in snow season, and check road status before setting out if there's been recent heavy snowfall.

Best Time to Make the Drive

Early morning departures (7-9 AM) from Srinagar give you the clearest light for photography along the route and get you to Gulmarg before the Gondola queues build up. In winter, mornings also tend to have the clearest skies before afternoon cloud cover rolls in over the mountains.

Tip: If you're combining Gulmarg with other stops, note that Sonamarg is in the opposite direction from Srinagar (northeast vs Gulmarg's southwest), so trying to fit both into one day is a long, tiring drive — better to treat them as separate day trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Gulmarg from Srinagar?

Gulmarg is approximately 51 km from Srinagar, and the drive typically takes 1.5 to 2 hours by road depending on traffic and weather conditions.

Is there a direct bus from Srinagar to Gulmarg?

Yes, J&K SRTC operates buses from Srinagar (TRC) to Gulmarg, though shared or private taxis are far more common and convenient for tourists given limited bus frequency.

How much does a taxi from Srinagar to Gulmarg cost?

A one-way private taxi from Srinagar to Gulmarg typically costs ₹1,800-2,800 depending on the vehicle type and season, while a shared taxi can cost significantly less per person.

Is the Srinagar to Gulmarg road safe in winter?

The road is generally maintained and cleared regularly in winter since Gulmarg is a major ski destination, but heavy snowfall can cause temporary closures or require chains/snow tires. Check conditions before departing during a snowfall warning.

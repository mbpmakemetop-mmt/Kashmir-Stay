Best Time to Visit Gulmarg Month by Month

Gulmarg looks completely different depending on when you go — waist-deep powder snow in January, blooming meadows in June, or golden larches in October. Here's exactly what to expect each month, so you pick the right season for what you actually want to do.

Gulmarg sits at roughly 2,650 meters, and its weather swings hard between seasons — which is exactly why "best time to visit" depends entirely on whether you're chasing snow, quiet meadows, or a middle-ground shoulder season with fewer crowds and lower hotel prices. Below is a realistic, month-by-month picture based on typical patterns, not brochure weather.

Month-by-Month Breakdown

December

Snow usually starts settling in by mid-December, though the first half of the month can still be patchy and unpredictable. Late December (Christmas week through New Year) is one of the busiest periods of the year, driven almost entirely by holiday travel rather than guaranteed snow — you can occasionally hit a dry spell right over New Year's. Daytime temperatures hover around 2-6°C, dropping well below freezing at night.

January

This is peak ski season. Snow depth is typically at its best, the Gondola (Phase 1 and Phase 2) runs consistently on clear days, and slopes are in prime condition. It's also cold — expect daytime highs around 0-4°C and nights well below freezing, so pack accordingly. Hotel prices are at their highest.

February

Often the best month overall for skiing: snow is still deep and fresh, but the New Year crowd has thinned out, especially on weekdays. Weather is marginally milder than January. If you want great snow without fighting for gondola tickets, aim for the first half of February.

March

Transitional month. Early March can still have good snow, especially at higher altitude near the Phase 2 station, but lower slopes start melting and can turn slushy by late afternoon. By the last week, meadows begin showing through the snow in patches.

April

Most snow at the base has melted, though higher elevations may retain patches into early April. This is when Gulmarg starts turning green — meadows, wildflowers beginning to bloom, and noticeably fewer tourists than winter. Daytime temperatures are pleasant, around 12-18°C.

May

One of the most underrated months. Full green meadows, clear mountain views, golf course open, comfortable weather (13-20°C), and a fraction of the winter crowd. This is a strong pick if you want Gulmarg without the ski-season chaos.

June

Similar to May but slightly warmer and busier, since it overlaps with school summer holidays across India. Still a good meadow-season choice, though book hotels a bit ahead.

July - August

Monsoon season. Expect intermittent rain, cloud cover that can block mountain views for days at a time, and generally muddy trails. Not the best window unless you specifically enjoy monsoon greenery and don't mind unpredictable weather.

September

Post-monsoon clarity returns. Skies clear up, meadows are still green, and it's noticeably quieter than summer. A good, slightly overlooked window for photography and hiking.

October

Larch and pine forests turn gold, temperatures start dropping (8-15°C daytime), and the crowd is minimal. Late October can bring the season's first light snow at higher elevations — a nice preview before winter properly sets in.

November

Cold, often bare of consistent snow until the very end of the month, and genuinely the quietest time to visit — many places take this as an off-season lull between autumn color and winter snow. Good if you want the destination almost to yourself, less good if you're expecting either bright greenery or reliable snow.

Booking tip: Hotel and houseboat prices in Srinagar (your usual base before heading to Gulmarg) swing heavily with season too. December-February and the May-June school holiday stretch are when prices peak — shoulder months like September-October often get you the same quality stay for noticeably less.

What About the Gondola?

The Gulmarg Gondola runs year-round weather permitting, but its usefulness changes by season. In winter, Phase 1 takes you up to Kongdoori for skiing and snow views, and Phase 2 continues to Apharwat Peak for more serious snow and skiing terrain. In summer, the same ropeway gives you green valley views and cooler air at altitude — a completely different, equally worthwhile experience. High winds or heavy snowfall can shut it down temporarily in any season, so build in a buffer day if the Gondola is central to your plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month to visit Gulmarg for skiing?

January and February are the best months for skiing in Gulmarg. Snow depth is at its peak, the Gondola runs reliably, and slopes are in the best condition for both beginners and experienced skiers.

Is Gulmarg open in summer?

Yes, Gulmarg is open year-round. Summer (April to June) offers green meadows, golf, and pony rides instead of snow, with pleasant daytime temperatures between 15-22°C.

When does it start snowing in Gulmarg?

Snowfall typically begins in mid-to-late December and can continue into March, though the heaviest and most reliable snow is usually from late December through February.

Is Gulmarg crowded in December?

Late December (especially around Christmas and New Year) is one of the busiest times in Gulmarg due to holiday travel, even though heavy snowfall isn't always guaranteed that early. January and early February tend to have better snow with slightly fewer holiday crowds on weekdays.

What Is Sinthan Top?

Sinthan Top is a high mountain pass located between Anantnag district and Kishtwar district in Jammu and Kashmir. The original Kashmir Stay guide gives its altitude as 12,500 feet and presents it as an offbeat alternative to more mainstream Kashmir destinations.

The attraction is primarily the landscape: high mountain terrain, open viewpoints and snow-covered peaks. For travellers who want a quieter and less conventional Kashmir experience, Sinthan Top can be an interesting addition to a South Kashmir itinerary.

Why Visit Sinthan Top?

Sinthan Top appeals to travellers who want a high-altitude mountain viewpoint rather than a conventional city sightseeing stop. Its scenery can feel especially striking when surrounding peaks are covered in snow.

  • 360-degree mountain views: The supplied guide highlights expansive views from the pass.
  • Snow-covered peaks: Snow views are described as a key attraction, including during late spring and summer.
  • Offbeat experience: It is presented as a quieter alternative to some of Kashmir's mainstream destinations.

Sinthan Top vs. Mainstream Kashmir Destinations

Sinthan Top serves a different travel intent from destinations such as Srinagar, Gulmarg and Pahalgam. Srinagar is stronger for lakes, gardens and city-based sightseeing; Gulmarg is known for meadows and winter experiences; Pahalgam is known for valleys and the Lidder River; while Sinthan Top focuses more strongly on a high-altitude pass and panoramic mountain scenery.

If your trip is short, prioritize the destinations that best match your interests. If you have more time, Sinthan Top can be considered as an additional offbeat stop when road and weather conditions permit.

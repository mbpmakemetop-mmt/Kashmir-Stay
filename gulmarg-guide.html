Welcome to Gulmarg

Gulmarg, located in the Baramulla district at an elevation of 8,694 feet, is Kashmir’s premier year-round alpine resort. Famous for its vast wildflower meadows in summer and world-class skiing slopes in winter, Gulmarg attracts travelers from across the globe.

Top Attractions & Experiences

  • Gulmarg Gondola: One of the highest cable cars in the world, split into Phase 1 (Kungdoor) and Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak at 13,780 feet).
  • Winter Sports & Skiing: Between December and March, Gulmarg becomes a hub for powder skiing, snowboarding, and snowshoeing.
  • Historical Landmarks: Visit Maharani Temple, St. Mary’s Church, and the historic Maharaja Cottage.
  • Drung Waterfall: A popular nearby cascade that completely freezes into ice cascades during peak winter.

Planning Your Gulmarg Journey

Distance: 51 km from Srinagar (approx. 1.5 to 2 hours drive via Tangmarg).

Seasons: April–June for temperate green views and blooming flowers; December–March for thick snow cover and ski activities.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gulmarg famous for?

Gulmarg is famous for its Himalayan scenery, winter snowfall, skiing, the Gulmarg Gondola, Apharwat Peak and year-round mountain tourism.

What is the best time to visit Gulmarg?

The best time depends on the experience you want. Winter is popular for snow and skiing, while the warmer months are suitable for meadows, sightseeing, hiking and other outdoor activities.

How do I reach Gulmarg from Srinagar?

Gulmarg is commonly reached by road from Srinagar through the Tangmarg route. Travelers can use taxis, private vehicles or other local transport options depending on current conditions.

What can I do in Gulmarg besides skiing?

Depending on the season, visitors can enjoy the Gulmarg Gondola, mountain sightseeing, hiking, meadow walks, photography, snow activities and other outdoor experiences.

Is Gulmarg suitable for families?

Yes. Gulmarg offers activities and sightseeing options for families, although winter activities should be selected according to weather conditions, age and individual experience.

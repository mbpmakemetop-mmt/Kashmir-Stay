Choosing a hotel in Kashmir — what to check

  • Location matters most: hotels near Dal Lake / Boulevard Road are best for sightseeing, while Lal Chowk and Rajbagh are closer to markets and offices.
  • In winter (December–February) ask about room heating, hot water hours and power backup.
  • Confirm whether breakfast is included and if the hotel can arrange airport pickup or local taxis.
  • Book early for April–June and the snow season — these are the busiest months in Kashmir.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

April to June is the most popular time, with pleasant weather and blooming gardens. September–October brings golden autumn colours, and December–February is snow season, ideal for Gulmarg skiing.

How do I reach Srinagar?

Srinagar International Airport has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai and other cities. From the airport, a taxi to most parts of Srinagar takes roughly 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.

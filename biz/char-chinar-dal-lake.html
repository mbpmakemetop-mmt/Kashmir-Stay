Taking a shikara ride on Dal Lake — what to know

  • Shikara rides are priced per boat (not per person) and by duration; agree the route and time before you start.
  • Sunrise and sunset rides are the most beautiful; the floating vegetable market runs early in the morning.
  • Popular routes include Char Chinar, Nehru Park, the floating market and the lake-side Meena Bazaar.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

Staying on a houseboat in Kashmir — what to know

  • Houseboats on Dal Lake and Nigeen Lake are reached by a short shikara ride from the ghat (jetty); ask the owner which ghat number to use.
  • Most houseboats have a front deck, a living room, a dining room and 2–4 bedrooms. Ask whether the rate includes breakfast and dinner — many do.
  • Evenings can be cold for most of the year. Check that rooms have heating or electric blankets if you travel between October and April.
  • Nigeen Lake is usually quieter than the busy Boulevard side of Dal Lake — choose based on whether you want calm or easy access to the market.

Staying in a guest house in Kashmir — what to know

  • Guest houses and homestays are usually family-run, offering home-cooked Kashmiri food and a local experience at a lower price than hotels.
  • Ask about shared vs attached bathrooms, hot water and Wi-Fi before you book.
  • Hosts are often the best source of local advice — ask them about taxis, markets and day trips.
  • In winter confirm heating; nights in Kashmir can drop well below freezing.

About Aynullah Tour and Travel

Aynullah Tour and Travel is a Kashmir tour and travel company based at Kawapora Payeen, Bandipora, run by proprietor Adil Hussain Bhat. We plan tour packages and provide comfortable vehicles for travellers who want to experience the valley safely, transparently and authentically. Being based in Bandipora, North Kashmir, we are close to Wular Lake, Gurez Valley and Lolab Valley — while also covering the classic Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg circuits. Whether it is a family holiday, a honeymoon or a group tour, we plan every trip with honest local guidance.

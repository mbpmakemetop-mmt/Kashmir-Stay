Booking a Kashmir tour with a travel agency — what to ask

  • Ask for a day-by-day itinerary in writing: which places (Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, Doodhpathri…) and how many nights in each.
  • Confirm what the package includes — hotel category, meals, cab type, sightseeing and airport transfers — and what is extra (gondola, pony rides, local union cabs).
  • In Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg, some local sightseeing must be done in union taxis; good agencies explain this upfront.
  • Check the agency's reviews and talk to them directly on call or WhatsApp before paying an advance.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

April to June is the most popular time, with pleasant weather and blooming gardens. September–October brings golden autumn colours, and December–February is snow season, ideal for Gulmarg skiing.

How do I reach Srinagar?

Srinagar International Airport has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai and other cities. From the airport, a taxi to most parts of Srinagar takes roughly 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.

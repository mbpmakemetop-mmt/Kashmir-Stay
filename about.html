Our verification process

Our standard is simple: a business must be real, trusted and genuinely operating in Kashmir. Government tourism registration is checked and shown where it exists, but many trusted local businesses operate without it, and we welcome them too.

1. Location

The business operates at the stated address in Kashmir.

2. Contact

Phone, WhatsApp, email and website are active and belong to the business.

3. Authenticity

How long it has operated, its reputation, and any documents the owner provides.

4. Govt. registration (if any)

J&K Tourism, GST or Ministry of Tourism registration is cross-checked and shown as a badge.

Ongoing monitoring: if a business gets repeated complaints or is reported by tourists, we investigate and may suspend or remove it. Verification confirms that a business is real. It does not guarantee service quality, so always read reviews and confirm details before you pay.

What Kashmir Stay is not

⚠️ Important. Please read.

Kashmir Stay is only an online directory and listing platform. We are not a travel agency, tour operator or hotel booking platform, and we do not sell or operate tour packages ourselves. Packages shown on Kashmir Stay are listed by the businesses that offer them.

All bookings, payments and service agreements are made directly between you and the listed business. Kashmir Stay does not handle tourist payments, bookings or reservations.

🚫 We are not responsible for:

  • Service quality: hotels, food, guides, transport or any service a listed business provides
  • Pricing: rates, discounts, hidden charges or pricing disputes
  • Availability: rooms, packages or sold-out services
  • Safety: personal safety, accidents, medical emergencies or theft during your trip
  • Cancellations and refunds: between you and the business
  • Misrepresentation: if a business gives false information or changes agreed services
  • Third parties: drivers, guides, staff or others connected to a listed business

Kashmir Stay earns from business listing plans, Spotlight, Premium and extra package slots, never from tourist bookings.

Tips for a safe booking

  • Read reviews and check for the ✔ Verified or Govt. Verified badge
  • Confirm price, dates, hotel names and inclusions directly with the business
  • Get written confirmation on WhatsApp, email or SMS
  • Pay by UPI or bank transfer instead of cash, and keep your receipts
  • Report any problem to us at support@kashmirstay.in and we will investigate the listing

Responsible travel in Kashmir

  • Respect local culture: dress modestly and ask before photographing people.
  • Protect the environment: don't litter around the lakes and meadows, and carry a reusable bottle.
  • Support local: choose Kashmiri-owned stays, buy from artisans and hire local guides.
  • Travel safely: follow official advisories and avoid restricted areas.

Frequently asked questions

Does my business get its own web page?

Yes. After verification, our system automatically creates a dedicated page for your business at kashmirstay.in/biz/your-business-name.html. It has your details, photos, reviews, map, and Call and WhatsApp buttons, plus structured data that helps Google understand your business.

Does Kashmir Stay link to my website?

Yes. If you add your website, your business page includes a direct, followed link to it from kashmirstay.in.

How do I add rooms or tour packages?

Open your listing in the app and tap the green button. Hotels, houseboats and guest houses get 🛏️ Rooms; travel agencies get 🧳 Packages. The first 3 are free, and you can buy extra slots in the app.

Does Kashmir Stay take commission from tourists?

No. Tourists contact businesses directly and book with them. We never add a commission to the price.

Is Kashmir Stay a travel agency?

No. Kashmir Stay is a directory and listing platform. Bookings and payments happen directly between the tourist and the business.

How do I report a problem with a business?

Email support@kashmirstay.in with the business name and what happened. We can't settle disputes or issue refunds, but we will investigate and act on listings that break our terms.

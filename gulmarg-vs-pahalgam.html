Gulmarg vs Pahalgam: Which Should You Visit First?

Both are essential Kashmir destinations, but they offer genuinely different experiences. If your itinerary is tight and you have to choose, here's what actually separates them.

What Makes Gulmarg Different

Gulmarg is Kashmir's answer to a proper mountain resort town — the Gondola (one of the highest cable cars in the world) takes you up to Apharwat Peak for genuinely dramatic snow-covered views, and in winter it's India's premier skiing destination. Even outside ski season, Gulmarg's meadows and golf course sit at a higher, more open altitude that feels distinctly alpine.

The trade-off: Gulmarg is more built around a specific set of activities (skiing, the Gondola, golf), the drive is on the steeper, more winding side, and prices — especially for accommodation and gondola tickets in peak winter — run noticeably higher.

What Makes Pahalgam Different

Pahalgam sits along the Lidder River and has a gentler, more pastoral character — it's the base for excursions to Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, and Chandanwari, and also the traditional starting point for the Amarnath Yatra. The pace here is slower: riverside walks, pony rides through meadows, and a town that feels less resort-like and more like a genuine Kashmiri valley town.

Pahalgam is generally easier on families, easier on the budget, and doesn't require the same altitude adjustment or winter-specific gear that Gulmarg does in ski season.

Which Should You Pick First?

Pick Gulmarg first if...

  • You're visiting specifically for skiing or snow activities
  • You want to ride the Gondola and see high-altitude alpine scenery
  • You're comfortable with a higher budget and don't mind cold-weather logistics

Pick Pahalgam first if...

  • You're traveling with young kids or older family members
  • You want a more relaxed, scenic pace rather than activity-driven sightseeing
  • You're visiting outside winter and want green valleys and river views
  • Budget is a bigger consideration
Honest answer: If your trip is 5 days or longer, you don't really have to choose — most itineraries comfortably fit both as separate day trips or overnight stops from Srinagar. The "which first" question mostly matters if you're tight on time or trying to decide which one to prioritize.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better, Gulmarg or Pahalgam?

Neither is objectively better — Gulmarg is better for skiing, the Gondola, and dramatic high-altitude scenery, while Pahalgam is better for gentle valley walks, river views, and a quieter pace. Your choice should depend on what activities you want.

Can I visit both Gulmarg and Pahalgam in one trip?

Yes, most Kashmir itineraries of 5 days or more include both, usually as separate day trips or overnight stops from Srinagar, since they're in different directions and not easily combined in a single day.

Which is more expensive, Gulmarg or Pahalgam?

Gulmarg tends to be more expensive overall, particularly in winter ski season, due to Gondola ticket costs and higher-demand accommodation. Pahalgam is generally more budget-friendly, especially outside peak season.

Which is better for families with young kids, Gulmarg or Pahalgam?

Pahalgam is generally easier for families with young children due to its flatter terrain and gentler pace, while Gulmarg's Gondola and higher altitude can be more demanding, though still manageable with older kids.
